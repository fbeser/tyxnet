# Third-party notices

Direct Go dependencies (verify transitive notices during each release):

- `golang.org/x/crypto` — BSD-3-Clause
- `gopkg.in/yaml.v3` — MIT and Apache-2.0 portions
- `modernc.org/sqlite` and its transitive packages — BSD-style licenses; SQLite

No Wintun binary or source is distributed. Future packaging must review the then
current Wintun license and redistribution requirements from its authoritative
distribution before bundling, preserve required notices, and document driver
provenance/version. Run dependency license and vulnerability scans before release.
